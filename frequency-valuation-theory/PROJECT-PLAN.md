## Charter

This paper extends the ODR thesis's Compton-counting ontology with a focused investigation of the claim that frequency — understood in dimensionless Planck units — is not an Archimedean real number but an element of Q: a rational ratio of two integer counts. The Compton frequency of a fundamental particle is thus a valuation-theoretic object with completions at every place per Ostrowski's theorem, not a "measured real quantity" with Archimedean error bars. This reframes particle identity as prime spectrum and dissolves the "thingness" of fundamental particles into recurring distinction-patterns carrying a fixed prime factorization.

### Core Claim (5-point)

1. **Frequency is a rational number.** In dimensionless Planck units, any physical frequency reduces to count-of-oscillations / count-of-Planck-times ∈ Q.
2. **The Compton frequency is the particle's invariant identity.** ν_C = mc²/h → dimensionless ω_C = m/m_P ∈ Q, a valuation-theoretic object.
3. **The Archimedean continuum is a shadow.** The Lorentzian manifold is the ∞-completion's projection of rational ratios living on Bruhat-Tits trees.
4. **Particles dissolve into periodic distinction-patterns.** No "thing" has a frequency — the frequency IS the identity. Identity = prime spectrum.
5. **Quasiparticles lack universal prime signatures.** Environmental frequencies vs. universal rational invariants.
